After about four years in California we moved to Utah. We lived in Lehi at first and now we live in Draper.

We haven't been in this ward long, but I hope that as we live out our faith here alongside the brothers and sisters of this ward, our family will learn and grow a great deal.

오늘 제가 말씀드릴 주제는 “연차대회를 통한 하나님의 은혜”입니다.

My topic today is "God's Grace Through General Conference."

10

연차대회는 우리 교회에서 매년 4월과 10월에 열리는 특별한 모임입니다. 전 세계의 성도들이 선지자와 사도들을 비롯한 교회 지도자들의 말씀을 들으며 주님의 뜻을 배우는 시간입니다.

General conference is a special meeting the Church holds every April and October. It's a time when Saints all over the world listen to the prophet, the apostles, and other Church leaders, and learn the will of the Lord.

11

저는 연차대회가 바쁜 일상에서 잠시 멈추어, ‘주님께서 지금 나에게 어떤 말씀을 주고 계실까?’ 생각해 볼 수 있는 특별한 시간이라고 생각합니다.

I think of conference as a special time to pause in the middle of a busy life and ask, "What is the Lord saying to me right now?"

12

하지만 우리는 매년 연차대회를 기다리면서도, 때로는 바쁜 일상과 지친 몸 때문에 그 의미를 충분히 마음에 담지 못할 때가 있습니다.

But even though we look forward to conference every year, there are times when everyday life is so busy, and we're so tired, that we can't absorb all of it.

13

솔직히 말씀드리면, 마음의 준비 없이 연차대회를 맞이했을 때는 말씀을 들어도 그 의미가 와지 않았던 경험이 있습니다.

To be honest, when I've come to conference without preparing my heart, I've listened to the talks and they just didn't sink in.

14

특히 아이들을 키우면서 연차대회 말씀을 온전히 집중해서 듣는다는 것은 쉬운 일이 아닙니다.

When you're raising children, giving conference your full attention is not easy.

15

아이들을 챙기다 보면 흐름도 깨지고, 과연 내가 지금 말씀을 제대로 듣고 있긴 한 건가 싶을 때도 많습니다.

You're looking after the kids, you lose the thread, and often you wonder whether you're really hearing it at all.

16

하지만 대회를 앞두고 삶의 작은 질문과 고민을 들고 기도하며 준비할 때, 연차대회는 다르게 다가옵니다.

But when we prepare for conference by bringing the small questions and worries of our lives to the Lord in prayer, conference comes to us differently.

17

저 역시 제 힘으로는 받아들이기 힘든 일을 겪으며, 주님께 답을 구했던 시간이 있었습니다.

3년 전, 저희 어머니께서 폐암 3기 진단을 받으셨을 때가 바로 그런 순간이었습니다.

I have been through something I couldn't accept on my own, a time when I went to the Lord looking for answers. It was three years ago, when my mother was diagnosed with stage 3 lung cancer.

소식을 들었을 때 제 마음은 참 막막하고 무거웠습니다.

‘왜 하필 우리 엄마일까? 왜 이런 시련을 주실까?’

When I heard the news, my heart was heavy, and I didn't know what to do. "Why my mom, of all people? Why would He give her a trial like this?"

19

또 병이 초기에 발견된 것도 아니고 이미 많이 진행된 후에 알게 되었다는 사실이 너무 속상했습니다.

하나님께 서운한 마음이 들었고, 솔직히 원망하는 마음도 있었습니다.

It also hurt so much that it hadn't been caught early; by the time we found out, it was already far along. I felt let down by God, and honestly, part of me resented Him.

저는 하나님을 믿는다고 하면서도, 막상 제가 이해할 수 없는 일이 생기자 하나님께 많은 질문을 하게 되었고

그런 마음으로 연차대회를 맞이했습니다.

I said I believed in God, but when something happened that I couldn't understand, I found myself asking Him a lot of questions. That was the heart I brought to conference.

‘하나님께서 내 질문에 내가 원하는 방식으로 답해 주시지는 않더라도, 내가 이 일을 혼자 감당하도록 내버려 두시는 것은 아니구나.’

Then, as I listened to one of the talks, this thought came into my heart: "God may not answer my questions the way I want Him to, but He is not leaving me to carry this alone."

Then I heard a talk by Elder D. Todd Christofferson, and it really stayed with me.

우리가 삶에서 피할 수 없는 힘든 일을 겪고 그 이유를 다 이해하지 못할 때에도, 하나님께서는 우리를 잘 알고 계시고, 구주께서 그 어려움 가운데 늘 함께하신다는 메시지였습니다.

His message was that even when we go through hard things in life that we can't avoid, and can't fully understand why, God knows us well, and the Savior is always with us in the middle of those difficulties.

어머니의 병이 사라진 것도 아니었고, 앞으로 어떻게 될지도 알 수 없었습니다.

But something small changed in my heart. The problem wasn't solved, but the way I looked at it was different. I believe that was one of the ways I experienced God's grace at that time.

I'm grateful to say that my mother is coming through her treatment well.

우리는 흔히 병이 낫고 상황이 좋아질 때 하나님의 은혜를 느낍니다.

We usually feel God's grace when an illness is healed and things get better.

Of course, that is precious grace too. But the grace of the gospel that I experienced was the strength and love that come through Jesus Christ, so that we can get back up even in the moments we can't handle on our own.

I didn't get every answer, and the illness didn't vanish overnight. But even when I had no answers, I was given the strength to lean on the Lord and keep going, one step at a time.

I learned that grace sometimes comes as a problem taken away, and sometimes it comes as the Lord staying with us to the very end, so that we can bear the problem and come through it.

‘주님, 제가 지금 꼭 알아야 하는 것이 무엇인지 알려주세요.’ 라고 기도하게 되었습니다.

I've also learned that the answer doesn't always come as one clear sentence. Sometimes it comes as a talk I keep thinking about, or as peace in my heart, or as a small thing I realize I need to do now.

In section 21 of the Doctrine and Covenants, there is a promise given to those who receive the words of the prophet.

I believe that, just as this verse says, the messages of general conference can be more than "good talks" to listen to. They can be a spiritual power that guides and protects our lives. And I don't think that power ends the moment conference ends.

At general conference last April, President Oaks taught that when we prayerfully study the messages we received at conference and live those principles, the Lord will keep giving us personal revelation and guidance.
