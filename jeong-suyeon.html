사랑하는 형제자매여러분, 저는 정수연 자매입니다. 최근에 오하이오주 신시내티에서 이사왔고 저희 남편은 타드형제이고, 아들 지호와 딸 지민이가 있습니다.

Dear brothers and sisters, I am Sister Jeong Su-yeon. We recently moved here from Cincinnati, Ohio. My husband is Brother Todd, and we have a son, Jiho, and a daughter, Jimin.

Forty years ago I took the missionary discussions from sister missionaries and was baptized, and I have been active in the Church ever since.

Not long ago I learned that one of those sister missionaries is in our ward. She is none other than Sister Jin-hyun. Words are not enough to say how glad I am to see her here.

Since moving into the Midvale Korean Ward, I have found people I had missed from my days in New York and New Jersey, and even people who guided me through my first years in the Church, and that makes me so happy.

Through Sister Kim Soon-hee I have also heard news of the Young Women president who set such a fine example for me and taught me so well when I was a Beehive, after my baptism forty years ago.

아마도 제가 미드베일한인와드로 이사온 것은 주님께서 저를 이 곳으로 인도하셨다는 느낌이 듭니다.

I have a feeling that perhaps my move to the Midvale Korean Ward was the Lord leading me here.

My topic today is receiving the Lord’s grace through general conference.

As we prepare lessons, as we study the scriptures each week with Come, Follow Me, and as we study conference talks in our classes, we encounter the words of the prophets, the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, the First and Second Quorums of the Seventy, and the leaders in the Relief Society and Primary presidencies.

그 말씀들은 그 분들이 주님께 봉사한 경험에서 나온 간증과 예수그리스도의 속죄에 한없는 감사와 은혜로움을 느끼게 합니다.

Those words let us feel the testimony they gain from their experience serving the Lord, and boundless gratitude for the Atonement of Jesus Christ and the grace it holds.

During the COVID pandemic, it felt as if the whole world had been shut in. We had to take the sacrament in our own homes, and family members took turns preparing the sacrament talks as well.

We have pulled from conference talks given up to now and used them in our weekly lessons, just as we set aside food storage against hard times.
